Off-screen activity

Make a three-panel story

Paper and crayons are enough. Try this before or after storytime.

Beginning, middle, end

  1. Draw a character and give them something to find.
  2. In the middle picture, draw one surprise or obstacle.
  3. Draw how it ends. Then point to each picture and tell the story out loud.
